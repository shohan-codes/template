# Invitation / design 01

**Family:** [PRD-0002](../../README.md). **Status:** Proposed; no implementation.
**Reference:** [E2](../../../../initial-component-references.md#e2-invitation-empty-state-with-avatar-row).
**Proposed implementation path:** `packages/pattern/src/empty-state/invitation/01.tsx`.

## Anatomy and original treatment

Optional people-related media, heading, short explanation and caller-owned
create-link/invite actions. Prefer original simple silhouettes or cleared assets;
do not replicate the source portrait-row composition. Decoration is not necessary
for the component to make sense and is hidden from assistive technology.

## Requirements

- Reuse the family's action and text contract. Do not add a second empty-state
  engine just for illustration styling.
- Invite and create-link controls emit callbacks; neither creates or sends
  anything automatically. Demonstrate opening and canceling a mock dialog.
- Meaningful instructions remain in text. Broken or omitted decorative media
  must not erase the title/actions or change their reading order.
- Support embedding inside an existing page section with a suitable heading
  level. No unconditional `main`, `h1` or mount-time focus movement.

## Design-specific checks

Verify no media, failed media, long labels, action stacking, RTL, pending action,
dialog cancellation and focus return. Inspect clipping at 320/375 px and large
zoom. Apply all [family acceptance](../../spec.md#acceptance). No source portrait
rights or exact proprietary demo-source access are implied.
